SETUP

Study information

Before you judge bias, fix the target. A RoB 2 assessment is made for one specific result of one specific outcome in one trial — not for the trial as a whole. Write down exactly which numerical result you are assessing, then choose which effect you are interested in: the effect of assignment to intervention (the intention-to-treat question most systematic reviews ask) or the effect of adhering to intervention (per-protocol). Your choice changes the Domain 2 signalling questions.

OVERALL

Overall risk of bias

How the overall judgement works. The result is at low overall risk only if all five domains are low. It is high risk if any domain is high, or if several domains have “some concerns” in a way that substantially lowers your confidence in the result. Otherwise the overall judgement is some concerns. The algorithm proposes the mapped judgement; you remain the assessor and may override it — always with a written justification.

    ANALYSIS

    Built-in analysis & draft summary

    What this panel does. As you answer, the tool re-runs the official RoB 2 mapping algorithms, explains why each judgement was proposed, checks your assessment for gaps and inconsistencies, and drafts a risk-of-bias paragraph you can edit and paste into a review or manuscript. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
